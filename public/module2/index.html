1. Priority Score Formula ($P_{score}$)
P_score = [(W_cat × 0.5) + (D_zone × 0.3) + (log10(U_count + 1) × 2.0)] × (Reputation / 100.0)

$W_{cat}$: Safety=10.0, Blockage=7.0, Minor=4.0, Aesthetic=2.0 • $D_{zone}$: 200m density count • $U_{count}$: Citizen upvotes • Reputation: Trust multiplier (fake reporters discounted).

2. Spatial Match Score Formula ($S_{dispatch}$)
S_dispatch = Performance_Score (0 - 100) / [Distance_km (Haversine) + 0.1]

Filters candidate crews with matching trade capability and availability status. The crew with the highest $S_{dispatch}$ receives the job assignment with a 24-hour SLA.
